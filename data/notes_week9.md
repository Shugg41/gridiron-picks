# Week 9 — a reason for every game

Researched 27 October. The two numbers per game (sportsbook spread and
FPI) are in `data/opinions.tsv`. This is what is NOT in either of them.

**The lesson of the week, about my own model:** FPI does not know about
injuries. It rates a team on what it has already done, so wherever the
news has changed who is playing, FPI is stale and the market is not.
Four games are market-only below for exactly that reason.

## Thursday and Friday — these lock at kickoff, not Saturday noon

**1. CAR @ GB — Green Bay.** GB -3.5 at home, total 47.5. Both sides are
hurt on a short week. Green Bay is missing Micah Parsons (PUP), Zaire
Franklin (hairline shin fracture) and Sean Rhyan (high ankle); Carolina
is worse off up front, with right tackle Taylor Moton on IR, left guard
Damien Lewis's knee, and Jaelan Phillips questionable. Covers leans
Carolina with the points. Take Green Bay, expect it close and low.

**2. BAY @ UCF — UCF.** Baylor fired Dave Aranda on Sunday at 40-40;
Joe Klanderman is interim, and QB DJ Lagway turned an ankle against
Auburn. Books cannot agree on the side — Big 12's own page has Baylor
-1.5, DocSports has UCF -2.5. A coin flip, so take the side that is not
in upheaval.

## Saturday — the games where the news decides it

**15. OSU @ USC — Ohio State. Market only.** Julian Sayin is out with a
throwing-shoulder injury and Ryan Day is planning for a possible
absence through the rest of the season; no starter named between Luke
Fahey and Tavien St. Clair. The market has absorbed it — the line went
7.5 to 6.5 and back to about 7. FPI has not, and still rates Ohio State
as though Sayin is playing, which is why the blend said 70% and the
price says 65%.

**10. NW @ ORE — Oregon, and it is the shakiest favourite on the board.
Market only.** Dante Moore is gone for the season, Dylan Raiola threw
for 246 yards with no touchdowns and two fumbles in the loss at
Illinois, the offence was shut out after half time, and Oregon fired
its first-year offensive coordinator the next day. Raiola has said the
playoff is "probably out of reach". Northwestern is 6-1 and ranked.
Oregon -3.5 at home is the number; FPI's 64% is reading old form.

**4. ILL @ MD — Illinois. Market only.** Katin Houser broke his hand on
10 October, out four to six weeks. It has not mattered: freshman
Michael Clayton II started and beat Oregon 21-10 on 24 October.
Maryland is 2-5, 0-4, and just made its defensive backs coach interim
head coach. Illinois -2.5 is fair.

**7. KSU @ COLO — Kansas State, and this is the likeliest to go wrong.**
Avery Johnson left the Kansas game and missed Arizona State, day to day
with neck and shoulder issues. Barnett is on crutches, Duff has not
played, Knuth has a tweaked ankle, and a running back has been moved to
quarterback. They are still favoured by 2.5 on the road, so the market
thinks it knows. Colorado is 2-5 and gave up 598 yards last week.

**8. CONN @ AFA — Air Force.** AFA -2.5 to -3 at home; ESPN's own
predictor has them at 61%. I earlier told you the data said UConn —
that was a sign-flip bug in my code, not a finding. Covers' expert
likes UConn on the points, noting Air Force's Evan Medders is doubtful.

**5. ARIZ @ TTU — Texas Tech, but the momentum runs the other way.**
Tech is favoured by about 3 at home, and lost last week to an unranked
Cincinnati. Arizona beat Iowa State 39-3. Both are ranked. The price

**12. UTAH @ CIN — Utah, on the road, against a team playing well.**
Utah is 7-0 and up to #5. Cincinnati is unranked but receiving votes and
just beat Texas Tech. Road favourite against a team with a scalp.

**6. WIS @ IOWA — Iowa, in a rock fight.** Total 36.5, which tells you

**14. CLEM @ FSU — CLEMSON. Deviating from the price, on purpose.**

That is a talent-and-home-field price rather than a form one, and the

This is a straight-up pool, so the spread is irrelevant and what
matters is that the market gives Clemson about 36% to win outright.
Taken anyway, as the week's one deliberate deviation: a card that
matches the favourite everywhere cannot win a winner-take-all week,

**20. IND @ JAC — Jacksonville.** Favoured by 7.5 to 8.5. Daniel Jones

**21. ARI @ DAL — Dallas.** Dallas -6.5, coming off 204 rushing yards in

## The one deviation

Twenty-nine of thirty are the market favourite. That is not a strategy,
it is what happens when every piece of news turns out to be already in
the price — Sayin, Hurts, Houser, Jackson, Baylor's coach, all of it was
priced before I looked. Research made the card correct; it did not make
it different.

Game 14 is the exception, taken deliberately. Four other defensible
deviations were offered and declined: Cleveland at Pittsburgh (CBS picks
Cleveland outright), Arizona at Texas Tech (Tech lost to unranked
Cincinnati, Arizona won 39-3), Chicago at Seattle (the 5-2 side is the
underdog) and Atlanta at Tampa Bay (Tampa is 2-5).

## Where I was wrong this week

**CONN @ AFA was my bug, not the app's.** The spread was read off a
printed string and matched against ESPN's spelling of the home team;
when the spelling did not match, the code took the line for the away
side and flipped the sign. Fixed, and it now declines to guess.

**Kansas State was not a second instance of it.** I said it was. The
source wrote the same line from the underdog's side ("Colorado +2.5")
and I read it as the opposite. The code had that one right.
