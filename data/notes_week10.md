## The chaos list — six, his pick

**1. Illinois over Nebraska (board ILL 39%). Friday 8pm, locks at
kickoff.** Illinois has won three straight, Nebraska has lost three
straight including 17-10 to Washington, and Illinois has taken four of
the last five in the series. Nebraska is still laying 4.5 on the road.
The form and the number point opposite directions. The line has moved
toward Nebraska, which cuts against it — that is the one mark on the
other side.

**2. Oregon over Ohio State (board ORE 27%, market 32%).** Both
starting quarterbacks are gone. Ohio State is on a freshman who just
lost at USC; Oregon is on Raiola and won. Two backups is the
definition of a game nobody can price, and the board is five points
more confident in Ohio State than Vegas is. FPI's 76% on Ohio State is
worthless here — it does not know Sayin is hurt.

**3. Air Force over Army (board AFA 38%, market 43%).** Army is -3.5 at
home a week after losing 49-22 at Memphis. Air Force is 6-2 and leads
the series 38-21 in sixty meetings. Both run option attacks, so the
game is short on possessions — the fewer the drives, the less the
better team's edge gets to show. Line opened Army -6, now -3.5.

**4. Ole Miss over Georgia (board MISS 41%).** Opened Georgia -5.5,
now -3.5. Georgia is a week off an emotional 27-8 win over Florida and
this is a road night game in Oxford. Ole Miss loses by 24 as readily
as it wins, which is what makes it live.

**5. Penn State over Washington (board PSU 44%).** Washington's starter
has a rib injury and is only "likely". This is the one game on the
board where FPI takes the underdog outright, and the news lines up with
it rather than against it. If Brown starts, the 56% is wrong.

**6. Wyoming over UNLV (board WYO 30%, market 36%). Saturday
10:30pm.** Wyoming has won three straight and is playing its best
football of the year. UNLV's defence gave up close to 500 yards to
Northern Illinois last week. UNLV opened -7, now -6, with the money on
Wyoming.

## Left off, and why

- **Miami over Notre Dame (MIA 32%).** The #1 team at 8-0 as a 3.5-to-
  5.5 dog, and FPI has Notre Dame at only 58% against the market's 66%.
  A real spot, but it is two good teams in a close game rather than
  anything unexpected waiting to happen.
- **Syracuse over Clemson (SYR 47%)** and **USF over ECU (USF 44%)**.
  Both are coin flips. A deviation only buys ground when the room is
  piled on the other side, and on a 50/50 game it will not be.
